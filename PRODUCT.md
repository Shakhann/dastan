# ДАСТАН

## Product
Static Kazakh-language heritage site for remembering and learning about half-forgotten Kazakh figures, genealogy (шежіре), traditions, history, culture and language. Being prepared for a competition. Hosted on GitHub Pages; plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hash routes.

## Users
Kazakh-speaking school students, teachers, families, and competition judges. Mostly phones; judges on laptops. People arrive to read about a person or tradition, listen to words, and save items to a profile.

## Content and function that must stay
Sections: Басты бет, Ел тұлғалары, Шежіре, Салт-дәстүр, Тарих жолы, Мәдениет, Тіл, ою pages and the «Ою тап» game, profile, sign-in (Supabase), credits. Real archival and landscape photos with mandatory attribution and licence captions. No tests, XP or levels.

## Tone
Respectful, remembering, proud, not touristy. Real photos of real people are treated with dignity: never crossed by graphics, never cropped through faces.

## Brand commitments
Name ДАСТАН. Kazakh Cyrillic must render fully (ә ғ қ ң ө ұ ү һ і). Earlier user feedback: too few images was disliked; the site must be image-rich.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard use, reduced motion respected, 360px phones supported.
